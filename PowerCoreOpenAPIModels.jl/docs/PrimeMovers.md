# PrimeMovers

Prime mover of a generating unit: the engine, turbine, water wheel, or similar machine driving the generator. PVe is photovoltaic, spelled to avoid colliding with the PV bus type.

## Allowed values
- `BA`
- `BT`
- `CA`
- `CC`
- `CE`
- `CP`
- `CS`
- `CT`
- `ES`
- `FC`
- `FW`
- `GT`
- `HA`
- `HB`
- `HK`
- `HY`
- `IC`
- `PS`
- `OT`
- `ST`
- `PVe`
- `WT`
- `WS`
